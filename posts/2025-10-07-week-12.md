October in the East Valley is absolute perfection. The high temperatures finally dipped below 95 degrees, and early morning walks with Cholla at Kiwanis Park feel crisp and refreshing.

At our 12-week checkup at MomDoc Tempe, we met with one of their Certified Nurse-Midwives. She listened to our Doppler heartbeat, reviewed my blood work, and spent 25 minutes discussing low-intervention birth preferences. Having consistent providers who take the time to listen makes such a difference.

My appetite is returning slowly. I have been craving fresh Medjool dates with almond butter and Sonoran avocados on sourdough toast. Starting to feel human again!
