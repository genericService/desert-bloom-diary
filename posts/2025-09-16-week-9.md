Today was our first in-person scan at MomDoc Tempe on S. Priest Dr. Walking into their office actually felt like stepping into a cozy living room rather than a sterile medical clinic, which immediately helped lower my baseline anxiety.

Alex held my hand tightly as the ultrasound gel was applied. A few seconds later, a tiny flickering image appeared on the screen. The sonographer turned on the audio, and the room filled with the fast, steady sound of a 165 BPM heartbeat.

The physician spent plenty of time answering all our questions, measuring baby at 9 weeks right on target, and confirming our due date of April 22, 2026. We left holding three little ultrasound prints, sitting in the car grinning like fools.
