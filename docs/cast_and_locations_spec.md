## 3. Style Transfer & Consistency Rules

1. **Color Palette**: Sunbaked Terracotta (`#c85a32`), Sonoran Sage Green (`#426c52`), Sand Dune Ivory (`#faf7f2`), Deep Ironwood (`#141312`).
2. **Real-World Reference Protocol**: When introducing specific East Valley landmarks (Tempe Town Lake bridge, Mill Ave shops, College Ave citrus trees), reference real photography or style-transfer existing reference shots.
3. **Lighting & Feel**: Soft warm morning or late-afternoon golden-hour desert lighting. Avoid synthetic flash or cold clinical overhead lights.
