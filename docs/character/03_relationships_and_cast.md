## 2. Maya & Alex Relationship Dynamics (Real Couple Realism)

### Recurring Domestic & Career Drama
- **Summer Thermostat Battles in 109° Heat**: Alex sets smart home automation routines for energy efficiency at 74°, while pregnant Maya, feeling intense heat at 109° outside, manually overrides the system down to 69°.
- **IKEA Nursery Drawer Assembly**: Assembling multi-drawer nursery storage without reading instructions, leading to leftover wooden dowels, backwards drawer slides, and shared laughter after discovering the mistake.
- **Client Deadlines vs. On-Call Pages**: Balancing Maya's tight freelance graphic design client deadlines with Alex's sudden late-night PagerDuty software engineering on-call incidents, requiring quick coordination for late-night coffee and dinner prep.
- **Studio Mess vs. Desk Space**: Alex leaves his half-empty espresso mugs on Maya's drafting table, while Maya leaves watercolor brushes near his laptop charger.
- **Going to Bed Upset**: Sometimes after a long day of heat, exhaustion, and financial worries about baby gear, they turn off the lights annoyed with each other without a neat Hollywood resolution.

### Healthy, Natural Resolution (STRICT NO-THERAPY-TALK RULE)
- **NO Therapy Speak**: Absolutely ZERO clinical HR/therapy buzzwords ("holding space", "validating boundaries", "processing triggers", "emotional labor", "active listening rubrics"). Real spouses don't talk like relationship podcasts.
- **How They Actually Resolve Conflicts**:
  - Making an extra cup of pour-over coffee or iced ginger tea in the morning without being asked.
  - Walking into the kitchen barefoot the next day and saying "I'm sorry I snapped at you last night, I was just really anxious about work."
  - Laughing at how silly they were being over IKEA drawer instructions.
  - Alex silently grabbing Maya's hand while sitting on the patio watching sunset.
  - A quiet hug in the hallway while Cholla sits between them.

## 3. Cholla (Rescue Dog)

* **Species/Breed**: Arizona rescue dog mix (desert wheat tan fur, soft floppy ears, dark expressive eyes).  
* **Rescue Origin**: Adopted from an East Valley rescue shelter in autumn 2022 after being found as a desert stray near the Salt River; named after the Sonoran desert cactus.  
* **Behavioral Quirks & Daily Habits**:  
  - **Bathroom Door Nudge**: Intuitively senses Maya's pregnancy early in Week 5, nudging bathroom doors open with her snout to check on Maya and resting her head on Maya's knees.
  - **Chin on Bump**: Rests her chin directly on Maya's growing pregnancy bump while Maya works at her design desk or relaxes on the couch.
  - **Nursery Furniture Inspector**: Sniffs and inspects all nursery furniture to approve baby Mateo's space.
  - **Clean Laundry Sock Thief**: Silently sneaks clean wool socks out of laundry baskets during folding sessions and carries them off to her floor bed.
